# Project+ Without The Bullshit

A CompTIA Project+ PK0-005 study guide for people who already know how to run projects and just need the cert. Every objective, the terms, the traps, nothing else.

## Why this exists

I have worked in the field for years, and I think the Project+ cert is dumb: it takes things people do every day and overcomplicates the language to seem more difficult than it is. I still need the paper, though, and the paper is gated by that vocabulary, so this guide translates it back into what the work actually is and says plainly where the objectives skip something the exam asks anyway.

And yes, I see the irony. I built a study guide for an exam that tests Agile by running the repo like an Agile project, an issue for every change, small pull requests, a CI gate, and merge when green, so I practiced the thing the exam wants me to define while building the thing that helps me define it.

- **Read:** the four domains in the sidebar, in order.
- **See it whole:** the [mind map](mindmap.md) puts the entire exam on one screen.
- **Memorize:** import [`anki/projectplus.csv`](https://github.com/ChiefGyk3D/ProjectPlus-Without-The-Bullshit/blob/main/anki/projectplus.csv) into Anki.

- **Print:** the [whole guide as a PDF](https://chiefgyk3d.github.io/ProjectPlus-Without-The-Bullshit/ProjectPlus-PK0-005.pdf), US Letter, built from these pages.

This guide covers every PK0-005 exam objective (1.1 through 4.5) in the official objective order, with a definition and an exam tip for each term. Learn the terms in order, then use the [gaps](05-gaps.md) and the [cheat sheet](06-cheatsheet.md) (formulas, acronyms, easily confused pairs) for last-minute review. The gaps section was cross-checked against the CompTIA Project+ All-in-One Exam Guide (PK0-005), including its exam tips and its Critical Exam Information appendix.

Exam format: up to 90 questions in 90 minutes, multiple choice, multiple response and performance-based items, passing score 710 on a 100 to 900 scale. CompTIA recommends 6 to 12 months of project management experience. The format and the domain weights above are from CompTIA's own [Project+ exam page](https://www.comptia.org/en-us/certifications/project/), checked 2026-10-21. The book agrees on the format and prints the weights only as an image.

### Study tips

- Most questions are scenarios. For each term, ask "when would a project manager choose this over the alternatives?"
- Learn lists in their groups (for example the four negative risk responses, the five conflict styles). Questions often give three right answers and one that belongs to a different group.
- Pay attention to words like *first*, *best*, *next* and *most likely*. They signal which step in a process the question wants.
- Memorize the formulas in the [cheat sheet](06-cheatsheet.md). A few questions need arithmetic, and all of them are quick.

**Memory aids used below:** PM = project manager, PMO = project management office, SOW = statement of work, WBS = work breakdown structure.
